Investment Thesis

AI 光纖 + 玻璃基板：雙引擎再加速

Corning 正在從「LCD 玻璃供應商」重新定位為 AI 基礎設施材料平台。光纖大單可見度已延伸至 2027；玻璃基板封裝是 2028 年後的第二條成長曲線。

S — Situation
  • FY2025 revenue +19% YoY to $15.6B; Q1 2026 +20% YoY — 4th consecutive double-digit quarter
  • Optical Communications segment grew +53.5% in FY2025, now 31% of revenue
  • Share price +307% trailing 12 months — market repricing GLW as "AI infrastructure"
  • Gross margin expanded 370bps from FY2024 to FY2025 (32.6% → 36.0%)
C — Complication
  • At $194, PE Fwd 45.9x — premium vs historical 15–25x; pricing Bull scenario today
  • Display Technologies (24% of rev): China LCD panel overcapacity = structural ASP headwind
  • Insider selling: COO, CTO, General Counsel all sold at $188–207 in May 2026
  • Glass substrate revenue: still pre-commercial; FY2028E contribution range $50M–$500M
Q — Question
  • Is 45.9x Fwd PE justified by AI fiber durability + glass substrate optionality?
  • Or is the market extrapolating a cyclical AI capex surge as a permanent structural re-rating?
  • What evidence would confirm vs deny the bull thesis at $194?
A — Answer
  • Base Case: WATCH — AI fiber demand is real and multi-year, but current price fully reflects the base scenario. Disciplined entry zone: $168–175 (MA50 support, ~13% below current)
  • Thesis upgrades if Q2 2026 Optical Comms YoY exceeds 35% and glass substrate gets named Intel design-win
  • Thesis breaks if optical comms growth falls below 15% YoY or Display mass impairment
